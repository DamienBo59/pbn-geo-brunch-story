faq:
  - question: "How do you make homemade granola really crunchy?"
    answer: "Bake it at 150 °C for 35 minutes in a pressed-down layer one centimetre thick, then let it cool completely on the tray without touching it. The crunch appears as it cools, when the sugar in the honey crystallises and welds the oats together. Use 300 g of oats, 80 g of honey and 50 g of oil."
  - question: "Why is my granola not crunchy?"
    answer: "Almost always because it was stirred or tasted while still warm, before the sugar had crystallised. The other causes are too thick a layer, which traps moisture in the middle, or jarring it while still warm: the steam softens it overnight."
  - question: "How long does homemade granola keep?"
    answer: "About ten days in an airtight jar at room temperature, provided it was completely cold when stored. After that it is still edible but loses its crunch. It freezes very well for three months."
  - question: "Can it be made with no sugar at all?"
    answer: "With no sweetener the oats do not stick together and the result is loose toasted oats, not granola. The minimum for clusters is around 50 g of honey or syrup per 300 g of oats, a third less than this recipe."

A good bag of granola costs between 4 and 7 euros for 400 grams. The homemade version comes to **around 2.50 euros for 600 grams**, and above all it lets you choose the sugar level, which climbs fast towards 20 % in supermarket brands.

The only real skill fits in one sentence: **do not touch the granola until it is cold**.
